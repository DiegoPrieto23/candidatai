## Tone
<!-- Formal, friendly, direct…? -->
Friendly but not chatty. Short sentences, active voice, first person.

## Structure
<!-- How you organise a text: what goes first, paragraph length, lists or prose. -->
What I bring first, then why. Paragraphs of three or four lines at most.

## Words I use
<!-- Verbs and phrases that come naturally to you. -->
- "build", "automate", "measure", "ship to production"
- "data quality" rather than "data excellence"

## Words I avoid
<!-- What you would never say. -->
- "passionate", "rockstar", "synergies", "ninja"
- buzzwords when a plain word exists ("leverage", "circle back")
