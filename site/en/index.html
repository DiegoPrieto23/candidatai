How it works

You decide. Your agent reasons. The app checks.

Every step has an owner, and your agent can only fill strict schemas that the code validates.

  1. 01

    Your profile, once

    Your agent creates it from your CV and what you tell it. It's the only source of what goes in a CV.

    • You
    • Your agent
  2. 02

    Jobs come in

    Paste a link, follow your companies' job boards, read your email alerts or let your agent search. Duplicates merge on their own.

    • The app
    • Your agent
    • You
  3. 03

    See how well they fit, and decide

    Your agent reads each job and reasons about the fit; the app applies your filters and scores it from 0 to 100. You approve or discard with one key.

    • Your agent
    • The app
    • You
  4. 04

    A tailored, verified CV

    Your agent writes it from your profile only, a separate verifier checks it and the app renders it in your template. Changing the design doesn't need the agent.

    • Your agent
    • The app
  5. 05

    The application, up to your click

    The app fills the Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or generic form, shows where each answer comes from and stops. It only sends when you type the company's name.

    • The app
    • You
  6. 06

    Every process, up to the offer

    A board that only moves with recorded steps. The app reads replies from your mailbox, your agent sorts them and prepares your interviews, and you accept each step.

    • The app
    • Your agent
    • You

What isn't in your profile doesn't go in your CV

Every line of a CV must point to evidence in your profile, or the code rejects it. Whichever agent you use.

  • A separate verifier checks every CV and cover letter before you get them.
  • What the job asks for and you don't have stays a gap; it isn't filled in.
  • Posting text is only data: hidden instructions aimed at an AI are flagged.
  • Messages and emails are drafts you send yourself.

Every feature, page by page

LinkedIn doesn't allow bots. Your click is fine.

LinkedIn doesn't allow bots or AI agents to browse its site, and CandidatAI respects that: no scraping and no automated applications. For its jobs there's the "Send to CandidatAI" button, a browser extension that only acts when you press it.

  1. Open the job on LinkedInSigned in, as usual.

  2. Press "Send to CandidatAI"The extension reads that page and nothing else.

  3. The job lands in your inboxIf it already came in as a summary from an email alert, it's completed.

  • It doesn't read cookies, browse pages or run in the background.
  • It can only talk to your app, at 127.0.0.1:8000.
  • For Chrome, Edge and Brave; the app guides you to load and connect it.
  • It also works on InfoJobs, Indeed, Tecnoempleo and any company's careers page.

LinkedIn job alerts by email come in on their own too. Letting your agent search LinkedIn is off by default: only you can turn it on, at your own risk.

Your data, on your computer

No accounts and nobody's servers: the app runs on your computer and only you decide what leaves it.

  • On your computer

    Profile, jobs, CVs and processes are kept in a folder on your computer. The app only listens on 127.0.0.1.

  • Your agent, your choice

    What your agent reads is processed by its provider, with your account and its terms. With a local model, nothing leaves.

  • What the agent never sees

    Your minimum salary and the amounts of an offer you receive are compared in the app: the agent never gets them.

  • Open source

    MIT licence: you can read every line that runs on your computer.

Install

Run it on your computer

  1. Run the installerNo Python, Node or admin rights. It downloads the latest release, checks its SHA-256 checksum and creates a "CandidatAI" shortcut.

  2. Open itA window starts the app and your browser opens at 127.0.0.1:8000, your own computer.

  3. Try the demo, then make it yoursCreate your profile from your CV and connect your agent. Start here walks you through it, with what each agent costs.

Latest release: v{{version}}. To update, run the installer again: your data isn't touched.

Windows 11, in PowerShell

Paste these two lines. To see first what it would do, add -WhatIf to the second one.

iwr -useb https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiegoPrieto23/candidatai/main/install.ps1 -OutFile $env:TEMP\cai-install.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File $env:TEMP\cai-install.ps1

Then open "CandidatAI" from the desktop or the Start menu.

macOS and Linux, in a terminal

No sudo. To see first what it would do, end it with | bash -s -- --dry-run.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiegoPrieto23/candidatai/main/install.sh | bash

Then open "CandidatAI" from ~/Applications on macOS or the applications menu on Linux.

Docker, optional

From a copy of the repository. Your agent stays outside the container, with some limits.

git clone https://github.com/DiegoPrieto23/candidatai.git
cd candidatai
docker compose up -d --build

Then open it at http://127.0.0.1:8000.

Good to know

It's a young project with one maintainer. This is what it doesn't do today.

  • Desktop onlyIt's made for a computer screen, not for a phone.
  • One person, one computerIt runs on your own machine, with no accounts and no sync between devices.
  • You need an AI agentWithout one you can review jobs, track processes and change CV designs, but not read jobs or write CVs, letters or guides.
  • English and SpanishIn the app and in the guides, only these two languages.

Questions

Something else? Ask it on GitHub.

How much does it cost?

The app is free and open source. What may cost money is your agent: a subscription you may already pay (Claude, ChatGPT or Google AI Pro), Antigravity CLI with a free Google account, or a local model.

Does it send applications on its own?

No. It fills the form and stops: sending takes your click, application by application. Messages to recruiters and follow-ups are drafts you copy and send yourself.

Can it make up experience?

No. Every line of a CV must point to evidence in your profile, and the code rejects any line that doesn't. A separate verifier also checks every CV and letter.

What data leaves my computer?

The app sends nothing to third parties without your confirmation. What your agent reads (your profile, the jobs) is processed by that agent's provider under your terms; with a local model, nothing leaves.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The installer brings everything it needs and creates a shortcut, and you use the app in your browser. Start here walks you through it, including installing your agent.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes. They have their own installer, like Windows, and the app opens the same way in your browser.
