Your whole job search, in one app

You decide, your AI agent reads and writes, and nothing is sent without you. From a new job posting to an offer: jobs from many sources, how well each one fits you, a tailored CV and cover letter with nothing made up, the application filled in and every process tracked to the end.

How it works

Everyone does their part

You decide, your agent reasons, the app stores, checks and automates. The agent can only fill strict schemas that the code validates.

Nothing made up

What isn't in your profile doesn't go in your CV

Every line of a CV must point to evidence in your profile, or the code rejects it. The app checks it, whichever agent you use.

  • A separate verifier checks every CV and cover letter before you get them.
  • Skills the job asks for that you don't have are marked as gaps, not filled in.
  • Posting text is only data: hidden instructions aimed at an AI are detected and flagged.
  • Nothing leaves without your click: messages and emails are drafts you send yourself.

Features

From a new posting to an offer

Jobs from many sources

A link or the text, your target companies' public job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and five more), your email alerts, a browser button or your agent searching the job boards you choose. Duplicates are merged.

An explained fit

Hard filters, a 0-100 fit that says where each point comes from, and your agent's reasoned verdict. Approve or discard from the keyboard.

A tailored CV for each job

With your template, photo and colour, and checked by a separate verifier. Ask for changes in plain words, edit it yourself or go back to any version.

Your own CV design

Import any CV (Word, PDF, an image, Canva) as a template, and change template, colour or sections in seconds without using your agent.

Cover letters and form answers

That match the CV and the job, from your own instructions.

Assisted applications

The app fills Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or generic forms and stops at captchas and logins.

Nothing leaves without your confirmation

Submitting takes your click, application by application. Messages and emails are drafts you send yourself.

Every process on one board

A board moved by recorded steps, a calendar, follow-up reminders and company replies sorted for you to accept.

Interviews and offers

A prep guide in PDF with a day-by-day plan, mock interviews, debriefs and company research with sources.

A funnel that tells you what works

Where each application stopped, by source, role, template and referral, and whether the fit score predicted what really happened.

Text aimed at an AI is caught

A job with hidden instructions is flagged and can never come out as a strong fit.

Useful without your agent open

Reviewing jobs, the board, the calendar, the funnel, your profile and CV designs all work without an agent.

Privacy

Your data, on your computer

No accounts and nobody's servers: the app runs on your computer and only you decide what leaves it.

On your computer

Profile, jobs, CVs and processes are kept in a folder on your computer. The app only listens on 127.0.0.1, your own machine.

Your agent, your choice

What your agent reads is processed by its provider, with your account and its terms. With a local model, nothing leaves.

What the agent never sees

Your minimum salary and the amounts of an offer you receive are compared in the app: the agent never gets them.

Open source

MIT licence: you can read every line that runs on your computer.

Install

Run it on your computer

No Python, Node or admin rights: the installer brings what it needs, checks the SHA-256 checksum of what it downloads and creates a "CandidatAI" shortcut. To update, run it again: your data isn't touched.

Windows 11

  1. Open PowerShell and paste these two lines: 
    iwr -useb https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiegoPrieto23/candidatai/main/install.ps1 -OutFile $env:TEMP\cai-install.ps1
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File $env:TEMP\cai-install.ps1
    To see first what it would do, add -WhatIf to the second line.
  2. Open the "CandidatAI" shortcut on the desktop or in the Start menu. A window starts the app and your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:8000.

macOS and Linux

  1. In a terminal, no sudo: 
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DiegoPrieto23/candidatai/main/install.sh | bash
    To see first what it would do, use | bash -s -- --dry-run.
  2. Open "CandidatAI" from ~/Applications on macOS (Spotlight finds it) or from the applications menu on Linux.

Docker (optional)

  1. From a copy of the repository: 
    git clone https://github.com/DiegoPrieto23/candidatai.git
cd candidatai
docker compose up -d --build
  2. Open it at http://127.0.0.1:8000. Your agent stays outside the container, with some limits: With Docker.

Then try the demo and make it yours: create your profile from your CV and connect your agent. Start here walks you through it, without being technical, with what each agent costs.

Maturity

Good to know

It's a young project with one maintainer. This is what it doesn't do today, or what hasn't been tested yet.

Desktop only

It's made for a computer screen; it isn't designed for a phone.

One person, one computer

It runs on your own machine, with no accounts and no sync between devices.

You need an AI agent

Without one you can review jobs, track processes and change CV designs, but reading jobs and writing CVs, letters or guides needs an agent.

What has really been tested

Windows 11 the most; macOS and Linux have an installer and pass the tests, but haven't been installed by hand yet. Claude Code, end to end. Details per agent.

English and Spanish

In the app and in the guides, only these two languages.

No scraping, no automated LinkedIn

By design: those jobs come in through email alerts, the browser button or your agent searching with your browser.
